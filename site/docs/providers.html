Provider catalogue

Providers are the scanners: each one knows where one family of disk usage lives, how big it is, and what cleanup is safe. devdoctor providers shows what is registered and available on your machine. Per-entry labels always win over the family default.

Platform notes

  • time-machine-local-snapshots and xcode-development-data need macOS. Everything else runs on macOS and Linux.
  • Providers report unavailability instead of failing: a missing binary or wrong platform shows up in devdoctor providers and in the report's diagnostics, never as a crash.
  • Unmeasurable entries (e.g. snapshot sizes macOS does not disclose) are offered with unknown size rather than hidden.
  • Project discovery is bounded to common code roots or DEVDOCTOR_PROJECT_ROOTS.
